
# Trovid.ai

### Turn Your Ideas Into Engaging Videos with AI.

Trovid.ai is an AI-powered video creation and editing platform designed to help creators, businesses, marketers, and professionals transform their ideas and digital content into engaging video projects.

From scripts and product pages to images, presentations, and existing video content, Trovid.ai brings creative workflows together in one platform.

- AI-powered video creation
- Transform scripts and ideas into videos
- Create videos from images and presentations
- Convert product pages and digital content into video projects
- Work with existing video content
- Create and edit video projects using AI-powered workflows

- Businesses and entrepreneurs
